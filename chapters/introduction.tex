\section{Overview}

Over the last two decades, mobile devices such as smartphones, tablets, and wearable technologies have become deeply integrated into everyday life, shaping people's daily habits and their interaction with technology and digital applications, almost replacing traditional desktop software and web browsing.
The adoption of smartphones has been driven by several factors, the growing computational power, battery increasing and wider functionlties compared to convettional computers due to their built'in sensors - such as gyroscopes, GPS moduels, lgiht sensors, cameras and vibtartion motors.
The presence of these features have allowed application to become create a more immersive user experience and provide different integration in different fields like healt, education and physical world - through sensors or camersas - 

A lot of examples of this trend can be observed in modern society. For instance, when creating an itinerary or even while driving, smartphones assist users through applications such as Google Maps and Waze. These applications do not merely provide directions, but also enhance the travel experience by offering real-time traffic updates, road event notifications, and real-time information about available parking lots. In this way, the sourranding environment has been enriched by ditigal detail, another notable example is Google Lens, which allows to identify objects from a photograph, or Shazam that can recognize a song through the device's microphone.
The technology integration has become a crucial key even in works environments, from the resturant that provide a digital menu by a tablet, to customers consulting shopping lists or mall stores. Similary, many museums allowing visitors to scan QR codes to access audio-guided tours or unlock Augmented Reality functionalities that enrich the overall experience. 




\section{Social networks}

Social networks such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, and Facebook have attracted billions of users worldwide, becoming an integral part of modern digital communication. These applications allow users to share diverse types of content—including photos, audio recordings, videos, and text messages—with individuals, groups, or even a global audience. This has expanded the possibilities for communication, collaboration, and the dissemination of information across personal, professional, and public contexts.

These platforms are primarily user-centric, relying on personalized algorithms that suggest content based on users' interests, interactions, and browsing habits. By facilitating the creation and maintenance of digital connections, social media has fostered increasingly interconnected social networks, bridging geographical distances and enabling communities to form around shared interests. Beyond communication, these platforms have also become channels for entertainment, news consumption, and even professional networking, reflecting their multifaceted role in contemporary society.
