\section{Concept}

GeoMedia is a social media based on goegraphic coordination, that allows to upload and share content as form of post, which serve as container for the information that users want to provide.

Moreover GeoMedia, covers several use cases

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Local tours}
    \item \textbf{Limitation visibility}
    \item \textbf{Catagorized content}: what the collection does
    \item \textbf{Sequentials posts}: what the collection does
\end{itemize}
